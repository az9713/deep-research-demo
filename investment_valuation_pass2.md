## 2. Per-Name Entry Framework

### GEV — GE Vernova  ·  VERDICT: WAIT / ACCUMULATE ON PULLBACK
- **Valuation:** $897.18, mkt cap $241B (Jun 10). Trailing P/E 26.93, **forward P/E 49.75**, **EV/EBITDA 70.64**. Forward P/E ~2x trailing is unusual (GAAP/one-off distortion in trailing net income; "growth" framing relies on adjusted forward EPS).
- **Re-rating:** Yes — EV/EBITDA of 70+ is extreme vs a mid-teens market. Richly valued, priced for substantial future growth.
- **Analysts:** Consensus Buy. 37-analyst avg PT $1,216.13 → +35.6% upside (a 21-analyst subset is higher at ~$1,259; a 24-analyst set ~$1,050). TipRanks Strong Buy (18/3/0).
- **BULL:** Grid + power-gen super-cycle backbone; analysts still see +35% to target.
- **BEAR:** EV/EBITDA 70x leaves zero room for execution slip; any growth-rate disappointment compresses the multiple hard.
- **KILL-RISK:** Multiple compression / priced-for-perfection.
- **Entry band:** Accumulate meaningfully on a pullback toward the high-$600s to mid-$700s (a >20% drawdown); current level offers thin margin of safety despite a good thesis.

### PWR — Quanta Services  ·  VERDICT: WAIT — TOO RICH (accumulate on a real pullback)
- **Valuation:** May 1 print $727.77 (vs 52wk high $728.85 — top of range); ~$679 by Jun 10. **Trailing P/E 95.48** with net margin just **3.67%**; LTM EV/EBITDA **43.98x** on $2.62B EBITDA. BofA values it on 29.5x 2027E EV/EBITDA (raised from 24x) to justify an $800 PT.
- **Re-rating:** Yes, sharply — stock +126% in the year to May 1; P/E ~95 vs ~3yr avg ~52, 10yr avg ~31. The multiple itself re-rated, not just the price.
- **Analysts:** Moderate Buy, 27 analysts (MarketBeat avg $733.87 → +8.1%; range $354 low outlier to $901 high). BofA Buy, PT $800 (~+10% from $727.77). Cross-aggregator targets vary ($678–$783).
- **BULL:** Record backlog ($48.5B); craft-labor/EPC is the least-substitutable node; BofA re-rated valuation basis to power-exposed peers.
- **BEAR:** P/E ~95 on a 3.67%-margin contractor; consensus upside only ~8% — the narrative is in the price.
- **KILL-RISK:** Backlog cancellation / margin disappointment + multiple compression.
- **Entry band:** Wait for a pullback well below ~$650; the 52wk-high entry offers little safety.

### FCX — Freeport-McMoRan  ·  VERDICT: BEST RELATIVE ENTRY of the verified set, but commodity-priced (accumulate on copper weakness)
- **Valuation:** Commodity-priced, NOT multiple-priced — copper price is the dominant profitability driver and "will either justify the valuation or expose it." Late-Apr: $60.57 at **8.10x NTM EV/EBITDA** — cheaper than SCCO (14.55x), in line with Rio (6.43x) / Glencore (6.20x). Now ~$64.1–$64.7 (Jun 9–10) after touching ATH ~$71.72 (Jun 2).
- **Sensitivity:** Each $0.10/lb copper move ≈ **~$400M annual EBITDA** at 2027–28 production (CFO, Q1'26 call; slide deck $390M). Management models 2027–28 EBITDA ~$14B at $5 copper → ~$21B at $7.
- **Analysts:** Apr 22 — avg PT $70.55 vs $70.36 price = ~fairly valued (range $48 low / -32% to $81 high / +15%, 22 analysts). TIKR mean ~$68, mid ~$71 (~12–17% upside from $60.57). **Live upside to ~$68 mean is only ~5–7% from ~$64.7.**
- **Technical caveat:** On Apr 22 it sat ~40% above its 200-day MA ($50.25) — momentum-stretched; management subsequently CUT 2026 copper guidance (to 3.1B from 3.4B lbs), the stock fell below $60, Morgan Stanley cut to Equal Weight (PT $66). The predicted pullback materialized.
- **BULL:** Cheapest copper multiple in the group; massive operating leverage to a copper up-cycle.
- **BEAR:** Earnings are a leveraged bet on copper; a copper reversal or further guidance cuts hit hard, and live upside to consensus is now thin.
- **KILL-RISK:** Copper-price reversal / production-guidance cuts.
- **Entry band:** Accumulate on copper-driven weakness (toward the high-$50s/low-$60s); avoid chasing near prior ATH.
